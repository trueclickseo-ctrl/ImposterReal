FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers for players, party hosts, teachers, and gamers.

What is the Imposter game?

Imposter is a free browser-based social deduction party game for 3 to 20 players. All players receive a secret word except the Imposter, who must bluff their way through clue-giving rounds without getting caught.

How many players are needed to play Imposter?

Imposter supports 3 to 20 players on a single device (pass and play) or across multiple smartphones and computers via room code links.

Can I play Imposter offline with paper?

Yes! Use our Resources page to download free printable cards and role sheets for offline play.
